Omer Kus

Machine Learning Engineer

I am a machine learning engineer at HUBX in Izmir, Turkey, where I work on image generation models — fine-tuning diffusion models such as Flux, Stable Diffusion, and Qwen image models, building training and inference pipelines, and implementing diffusion distillation methods (DMD2, LADD) for faster sampling in production.

Before that, I was a research assistant at MIRALAB (Izmir Institute of Technology), where I worked on diffusion model conditioning and multimodal generative models. I hold a BSc in Electronics and Communication Engineering from the Izmir Institute of Technology, where I graduated as department valedictorian.

Projects

Semantic-Guided Diffusion Data Augmentation MIRALAB research project An image-to-image augmentation pipeline combining Stable Diffusion 2.0 and LLaVA: captions generated by LLaVA guide the diffusion model to produce semantically consistent but visually diverse variants of training images.
Multimodal Few-Shot Aerial Scene Recognition MIRALAB research project Extended CLIP with few-shot audio-visual multimodality using the TipAdapter framework — combining audio features from scene recordings with visual embeddings to improve aerial scene classification beyond vision-only baselines.
